C18 is now the unchanged official C178373 / Panasonic 35SVPK330M import, **330 µF / 35 V**, replacing C2918361. The new part is connected provisionally; it is not a completed power/assembly approval. Direct supplier stock was 251 on 2026-10-03, and its native polarized-capacitor symbol, two electrical pins/pads, full Circuit JSON schema and five native checks pass. C16/C17/C29 DC-bias and current-sharing qualification remain open. The old capacitor's 150 mA rating at 120 Hz was insufficient evidence for high-frequency acceptance or rejection; the replacement supplies a manufacturer-documented PWM-frequency rating.

## Ripple and compensation screen

scripts/buck-output-review.py implements the TI TPS54360 §7.3.14–16 continuous-conduction equivalent circuit with explicit candidate profiles. Nine independent limiting-case comparisons against TI Eq.11 pass. The active C178373 profile uses its 18 mΩ specification and screens all 5/9/12 V rails with a deliberately broad 16,200-case sensitivity sweep. **Threshold failures remain; this is not an approved compensation design.** Exact results are in evidence/buck-output-C178373-A10-review.json. The earlier unused C133439 14 mΩ sweep (1,570 violations) remains historical in buck-output-review-A10.json.

The varied GM, ESR, delay and MLCC parameters are assumptions, not guaranteed part corners. Constant 0.1/0.6 Ω ESR models exceed either candidate's specification at 100–300 kHz and cannot describe actual full-band response. Crossovers above 48 kHz are outside this simplified model's design range; their phase margins do not establish hardware instability. Nominal plots are preliminary. A frequency-dependent capacitor/regulator model, DCM/light-load review and transient validation are required before changing or approving the network.

C178373's manufacturer frequency factor is 0.7 throughout 10–100 kHz. At approximately 20 kHz this gives **3.08 A** from the 4.4 A allowable 100 kHz ripple rating, conditional on the manufacturer's **Tx ≤105°C** limit. The conservative motor/buck screen below is about **2.467 A RMS**, leaving conditional PWM ripple margin. At 105°C < Tx ≤125°C the 100 kHz rating falls to **1.39 A** (0.973 A after the factor), so this is explicitly not a 2 A motor rating through 125°C. Lower-frequency startup/mechanical envelopes, mixed-frequency current sharing and the unspecified ≥500 kHz multiplier at the 600 kHz buck frequency still require qualification.
For C133439, manufacturer 10 kHz ripple multiplier 0.60 screens 5 A as 3 A; using this lower tabulated multiplier for approximately 20 kHz is a conservative engineering assumption pending manufacturer confirmation. A signed motor-current waveform can approach the 2.423 A current-limit screen at 50% duty; treating it only as a unidirectional I√D(1−D) load is not conservative. Buck triangular-ripple RMS is ΔI/√12. Combining the independent PWM and switching components gives approximately **2.467 A RMS**. This is below 3 A conditionally, but does not resolve lower-frequency mechanical envelopes/startup/reversal, waveform/current sharing, temperature, or the unspecified multiplier above 500 kHz at the current 600 kHz buck frequency.

No phase-margin, current-sharing, thermal, charger or motor measurement is invented. The board remains an untested prototype; placement and routing are disabled.
