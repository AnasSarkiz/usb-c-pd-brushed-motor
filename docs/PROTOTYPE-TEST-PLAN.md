# Motor-controller validation matrix

A22 review, 2026-10-04. **No physical tests have been performed.** Use only the source/hash-linked prototype and its bring-up firmware. Bring-up firmware remains inhibited until separately approved NVM, receive-provenance and measurement profiles are installed; successful compilation is not live PD qualification.

Initial motor tests require stored mechanical/electrical energy ≤1 mJ, a guarded load and no externally powered back-drive. Expansion to a 20 ms dump pulse, ≤0.5 J per event and ≥10 s between events requires measured clamp voltage, current-regulation overshoot and MOSFET SOA/temperature evidence first. Approximately 2 A continuous remains the design target, subject to measured temperatures and current limits.

## Setup and evidence

Record motor model and rated voltage, cold/hot winding resistance, inductance, no-load/startup/stall current, load inertia and external back-driving conditions. Use a PD analyzer/source emulator, current-limited bench setup, differential motor/VM probes, current probe, thermocouples and guarded load fixture. Never ground an oscilloscope lead to a motor terminal. Log board revision, cable/source capabilities, settings, captures, temperatures and fault results.

The provisional screening assumptions are 2 A continuous; the corrected analytical current-limit upper corner is approximately 2.423 A plus unqualified response overshoot, 0–40°C ambient and 125°C target junction temperature. They are not measured operating ratings. Qualify representative motors at 5/9/12 V rather than requiring one particular motor. Peak duration, 5 V current-limit accuracy and energy/thermal bounds require measurements. No dedicated timed-reversal circuit is added in A4; both rapid reversal tests are mandatory and determine whether a coast delay is needed.

## Control-state basis

[DRV8874 PWM mode](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/drv8874.pdf), PMODE high: IN1/IN2=00 coasts, 10 drives forward, 01 drives reverse, and 11 brakes through the low-side devices. nSLEEP=0 makes the bridge Hi-Z. The SPDT ON-OFF-ON routes timer PWM through common pin 2 to IN1 or IN2. Center opens both, and TI internal 100 kΩ typical pull-downs produce 00/coast. Direction LEDs follow PWM; check loaded timer VOH and brightness. Contact bounce may alternate drive/coast; center travel does not guarantee a stopping interval. The integrated driver generates MOSFET dead time; it is not an external reversal controller.

Reject a prototype that only passes netlist/build or no-load spin tests. Physical stage 7 remains not started until dated measurements are recorded for the declared conditions.


A11 discharge checks: with the motor disconnected and all three selected voltages, inhibit bridge/power and record VM, feedback gates and R68 temperature through full decay. Confirm measured VM≤1 V before branch changes and adequate margin below the 3 s timeout. Repeat at declared supply/temperature/capacitance extremes and rapid replug. Hold VM with external back-drive: timeout must latch inhibition, preserve old feedback and never enable. 9 V/15 V is prohibited by the current contract policy; verify rejection and adequate 20 V operation with a hot eFuse. No timed direction/reversal circuit is added.

## Reset, sensing and transport qualification

- Reset/watchdog/brownout forces both feedback GPIOs off, returning the buck feedback network to 5 V while hardware inhibits the motor and input. Unlike foreground shutdown, reset cannot retain the previous software-driven feedback state. Capture reset at charged 9/12 V VM, verify safe discharge and no re-enable before the VM interval qualifies. Test fault injection during the interrupt-masked commit window and peripheral timeout paths.
- Measure ADC reference/calibration, leakage, sequential-sample skew, acquisition settling, noise and total filter lag before approving a measurement profile. The linked runtime uses 32 MHz ADC clock and 160.5-cycle sampling; bind actual conditions to conservative outward-rounded intervals and oldest sample timestamps. Inject detach, stale samples, rollover and expired qualification.
- Verify I2C PB8/PB9 SCL/SDA rise ≤200 ns, fall ≤60 ns and bus capacitance within the documented timing envelope. Exercise NACK, stuck bus, timeout and partial reads; each must revoke permissions.
- Independently verify all 40 manufacturing NVM bytes through the manufacturer workflow, then capture fresh Source_Capabilities, request readback, Accept and PS_RDY with exact source/request generations. A RAM write or matching NVM is insufficient contract evidence.
- Record SWD image ELF/BIN hashes, real stack high-water, watchdog interval and complete ISR/foreground traces. The static stack screen is 948 bytes against 2048 reserved; no measured high-water exists.
