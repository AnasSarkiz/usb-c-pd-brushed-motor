# Motor-controller validation matrix

A9 review, 2026-10-03. **No physical tests have been performed.** This plan adds the requested cases; calculations and datasheet review are separately identified. Do not energize the current unapproved schematic. Use the eventual validated revision, programmed PD configuration and declared motor/load envelope.

## Setup and evidence

Record motor model and rated voltage, cold/hot winding resistance, inductance, no-load/startup/stall current, load inertia and external back-driving conditions. Use a PD analyzer/source emulator, current-limited bench setup, differential motor/VM probes, current probe, thermocouples and guarded load fixture. Never ground an oscilloscope lead to a motor terminal. Log board revision, cable/source capabilities, settings, captures, temperatures and fault results.

The provisional screening assumptions are 2 A continuous; the corrected analytical current-limit upper corner is approximately 2.423 A plus unqualified response overshoot, 0–40°C ambient and 125°C target junction temperature. They are not measured operating ratings. Qualify representative motors at 5/9/12 V rather than requiring one particular motor. Peak duration, 5 V current-limit accuracy and energy/thermal bounds require measurements. No dedicated timed-reversal circuit is added in A4; both rapid reversal tests are mandatory and determine whether a coast delay is needed.

## Control-state basis

[DRV8874 PWM mode](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/drv8874.pdf), PMODE high: IN1/IN2=00 coasts, 10 drives forward, 01 drives reverse, and 11 brakes through the low-side devices. nSLEEP=0 makes the bridge Hi-Z. The SPDT ON-OFF-ON routes timer PWM through common pin 2 to IN1 or IN2. Center opens both, and TI internal 100 kΩ typical pull-downs produce 00/coast. Direction LEDs follow PWM; check loaded timer VOH and brightness. Contact bounce may alternate drive/coast; center travel does not guarantee a stopping interval. The integrated driver generates MOSFET dead time; it is not an external reversal controller.

Reject a prototype that only passes netlist/build or no-load spin tests. Physical stage 7 remains not started until dated measurements are recorded for the declared conditions.
