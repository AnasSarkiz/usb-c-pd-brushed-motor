             Features/Benefits                                                       Typical Applications
             • Variety of actuators & terminations                                   • Test & measurement equipment
             • PC and panel mounting                                                 • Computer peripherals &
             • Shorting or non-shorting contacts                                       network products
             • RoHS compliant                                                        • Consumer products
             • Halogen free material

                                                                                                               Materials
             Specifications
                                                                                                               BASE: Phenolic resin.
             CONTACT RATING: See individual parts.
                                                                                                               ACTUATOR: Standard: POM, Black.
             MECHANICAL & ELECTRICAL LIFE: 10,000 cycles.
                                                                                                                          High Temperature Option: PA46, Black.
             CONTACT RESISTANCE: 20 m Ω or less.                                                                                                                                                              I
                                                                                                               HOUSING PLATE: Non-compatible; SPCC Sn plated.
             INSULATION RESISTANCE: 100 M Ω min. at 500 VDC.                                                       Compatible; SPCC Ni plated.
             DIELECTRIC STRENGTH: 500 VAC for 1 minute.

                                                                                                                                                                                                            Slide
                                                                                                               MOVABLE CONTACT: Copper alloy, silver plated.
             OPERATING TEMPERATURE: –30ºC to +70ºC.                                                            STATIONARY CONTACTS: Brass, silver plated.
             PACKAGING: Bulk                                                                                   TERMINALS: Brass, silver plated.
             * For RoHS compatible switches contact customer service.                                          TERMINAL SEALED.

                                                                                                               NOTE: Specifications and materials listed above are for switches with standard options.
                                                                                                               For information on specific and custom switches, consult Customer Service Center.
